abstract: The PC algorithm learns maximally oriented causal Bayesian networks. However,
  there is no equivalent complete algorithm for learning the structure of relational
  models, a more expressive generalization of Bayesian networks. Recent developments
  in the theory and representation of relational models support lifted reasoning
  about conditional independence. This enables a powerful constraint for orienting
  bivariate dependencies and forms the basis of a new algorithm for learning structure. We present the relational causal discovery (RCD) algorithm that learns causal
  relational models. We prove that RCD is sound and complete, and we present empirical
  results that demonstrate effectiveness.
