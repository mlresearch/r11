abstract: We consider the problem of learning Bayesian networks (BNs) from complete
  discrete data. This problem of discrete optimisation is formulated as an integer
  program (IP). We describe the various steps we have taken to allow efficient
  solving of this IP. These are (i) efficient search for cutting planes, (ii) a fast
  greedy algorithm to find high-scoring (perhaps not optimal) BNs and (iii) tightening
  the linear relaxation of the IP. After relating this BN learning problem to set
  covering and the multidimensional 0-1 knapsack problem, we present our empirical
  results. These show improvements, sometimes dramatic, over earlier results.
