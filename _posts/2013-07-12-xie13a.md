abstract: 'Document clustering and topic modeling are two closely related tasks which
  can mutually benefit each other. Topic modeling can project documents into a topic
  space which facilitates effective document clustering. Cluster labels discovered
  by document clustering can be incorporated into topic models to extract local topics
  specific to each cluster and global topics shared by all clusters. In this paper,
  we propose a multi-grain clustering topic model (MGCTM) which integrates document
  clustering and topic modeling into a unified framework and jointly performs
  the two tasks to achieve the overall best performance. Our model tightly couples
  two components: a mixture component used for discovering latent groups in document
  collection and a topic model component used for mining multi-grain topics including
  local topics specific to each cluster and global topics shared across clusters.
  We employ variational inference to approximate the posterior of hidden variables
  and learn model parameters. Experiments on two datasets demonstrate the effectiveness
  of our model.'
