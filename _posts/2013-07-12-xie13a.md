abstract: 'Document clustering and topic modeling are two closely related tasks which
  can mutu- ally benefit each other. Topic modeling can project documents into a topic
  space which facilitates effective document cluster- ing. Cluster labels discovered
  by document clustering can be incorporated into topic models to extract local topics
  specific to each cluster and global topics shared by all clus- ters. In this paper,
  we propose a multi-grain clustering topic model (MGCTM) which inte- grates document
  clustering and topic model- ing into a unified framework and jointly per- forms
  the two tasks to achieve the overall best performance. Our model tightly couples
  two components: a mixture component used for discovering latent groups in document
  col- lection and a topic model component used for mining multi-grain topics including
  local topics specific to each cluster and global top- ics shared across clusters.
  We employ varia- tional inference to approximate the posterior of hidden variables
  and learn model param- eters. Experiments on two datasets demon- strate the effectiveness
  of our model.'
