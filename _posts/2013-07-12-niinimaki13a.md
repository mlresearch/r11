abstract: Consider a collection of weighted subsets of a ground set N. Given a query
  subset Q of N, how fast can one (1) find the weighted sum over all subsets of Q,
  and (2) sample a sub- set of Q proportionally to the weights? We present a tree-based
  greedy heuristic, Treedy, that for a given positive tolerance d answers such counting
  and sampling queries to within a guaranteed relative error d and total vari- ation
  distance d, respectively. Experimen- tal results on artificial instances and in
  ap- plication to Bayesian structure discovery in Bayesian networks show that approximations
  yield dramatic savings in running time com- pared to exact computation, and that
  Treedy typically outperforms a previously proposed sorting-based heuristic.
