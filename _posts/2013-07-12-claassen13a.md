abstract: 'This paper shows that causal model discov- ery is not an NP-hard problem,
  in the sense that for sparse graphs bounded by node de- gree k the sound and complete
  causal model can be obtained in worst case order N 2(k+2) independence tests, even
  when latent vari- ables and selection bias may be present. We present a modification
  of the well-known FCI algorithm that implements the method for an independence oracle,
  and suggest improve- ments for sample/real-world data versions. It does not contradict
  any known hardness re- sults, and does not solve an NP-hard prob- lem: it just proves
  that sparse causal discov- ery is perhaps more complicated, but not as hard as learning
  minimal Bayesian networks.'
