abstract: The PC algorithm learns maximally oriented causal Bayesian networks. However,
  there is no equivalent complete algorithm for learning the structure of relational
  models, a more ex- pressive generalization of Bayesian networks. Recent developments
  in the theory and repre- sentation of relational models support lifted reasoning
  about conditional independence. This enables a powerful constraint for ori- enting
  bivariate dependencies and forms the basis of a new algorithm for learning struc-
  ture. We present the relational causal discov- ery (RCD) algorithm that learns causal
  rela- tional models. We prove that RCD is sound and complete, and we present empirical
  re- sults that demonstrate effectiveness.
