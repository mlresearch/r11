abstract: Many probabilistic inference tasks involve summations over exponentially
  large sets. Recently, it has been shown that these prob- lems can be reduced to
  solving a polyno- mial number of MAP inference queries for a model augmented with
  randomly gener- ated parity constraints. By exploiting a con- nection with max-likelihood
  decoding of bi- nary codes, we show that these optimizations are computationally
  hard. Inspired by iter- ative message passing decoding algorithms, we propose an
  Integer Linear Programming (ILP) formulation for the problem, enhanced with new
  sparsification techniques to improve decoding performance. By solving the ILP through
  a sequence of LP relaxations, we get both lower and upper bounds on the parti- tion
  function, which hold with high probabil- ity and are much tighter than those obtained
  with variational methods.
