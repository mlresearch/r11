abstract: Many probabilistic inference tasks involve summations over exponentially
  large sets. Recently, it has been shown that these problems can be reduced to
  solving a polynomial number of MAP inference queries for a model augmented with
  randomly generated parity constraints. By exploiting a connection with max-likelihood
  decoding of binary codes, we show that these optimizations are computationally
  hard. Inspired by iterative message passing decoding algorithms, we propose an
  Integer Linear Programming (ILP) formulation for the problem, enhanced with new
  sparsification techniques to improve decoding performance. By solving the ILP through
  a sequence of LP relaxations, we get both lower and upper bounds on the partition
  function, which hold with high probability and are much tighter than those obtained
  with variational methods.
