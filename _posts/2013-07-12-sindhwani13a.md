abstract: We propose a general matrix-valued mul- tiple kernel learning framework
  for high- dimensional nonlinear multivariate regression problems. This framework
  allows a broad class of mixed norm regularizers, includ- ing those that induce sparsity,
  to be im- posed on a dictionary of vector-valued Repro- ducing Kernel Hilbert Spaces.
  We develop a highly scalable and eigendecomposition- free algorithm that orchestrates
  two inex- act solvers for simultaneously learning both the input and output components
  of separa- ble matrix-valued kernels. As a key appli- cation enabled by our framework,
  we show how high-dimensional causal inference tasks can be naturally cast as sparse
  function esti- mation problems, leading to novel nonlinear extensions of a class
  of Graphical Granger Causality techniques. Our algorithmic de- velopments and extensive
  empirical studies are complemented by theoretical analyses in terms of Rademacher
  generalization bounds.
