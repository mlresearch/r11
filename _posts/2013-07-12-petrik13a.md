abstract: We propose solution methods for previously- unsolved constrained MDPs in
  which actions can continuously modify the transition probabilities within some acceptable
  sets. While many meth- ods have been proposed to solve regular MDPs with large state
  sets, there are few practical approaches for solving constrained MDPs with large
  action sets. In particular, we show that the continuous action sets can be replaced
  by their extreme points when the rewards are linear in the modulation. We also develop
  a tractable opti- mization formulation for concave reward func- tions and, surprisingly,
  also extend it to non- concave reward functions by using their concave envelopes.
  We evaluate the effectiveness of the approach on the problem of managing delinquen-
  cies in a portfolio of loans.
